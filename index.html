Not a rule of thumb

"About 400 pounds a hole" is a guess. The real number depends on your trailer: the weight the tandems carry, the hole spacing on the rail, and the kingpin-to-tandem distance. AxleSet takes your rig's measurements once and works every hole and every fifth-wheel notch from the geometry — then shows you what happens at each one, so you can see why the answer is what it is.

The checks the other calculators skip

  • Federal Bridge Formula (23 CFR 658.17) on every group of consecutive axles, including the 36-foot two-tandem exception. Verified cell by cell against the published table.
  • Kingpin-to-rear-axle rules — California's 40 feet and the other states that have one, including the ones that measure differently.
  • All 50 states: single, tandem, gross, bridge, and the fine schedule, each read on the state's own statute or DOT page and cited. If a state could not be confirmed from an official source, the app says not verified and falls back to federal — it never guesses.
  • Interstate vs. state highway where the limits differ, and the APU allowance.
