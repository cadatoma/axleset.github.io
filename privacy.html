Privacy Policy

AxleSet · Last updated 27 September 2026

AxleSet does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics and no advertising code in the app. AxleSet makes no network requests of its own; the optional iCloud backup uses your own Apple account.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your rig profile: the measurements and ratings you enter for your tractor and trailer, including the trailer's length.
  • The scale weights you type, the state you pick, the road you picked in it — Interstate, state highway, or one of the lower road classes a state writes — your slide strategy and drives target, pounds or kilograms, and whether you turned on the option that adds the tolerance a state's law grants as a right. That last setting stays only on this phone: it is not part of the iCloud backup below.
  • Saved scale tickets: weights, date, the place you typed, and the photo you attached.

All of it is stored on the device. We cannot see it, and AxleSet does not send it anywhere. If you back up your iPhone (iCloud Backup or Finder), Apple's backup includes this app's files, the same as for any other app; that copy is yours, under your Apple ID. Deleting the app deletes all of it from the phone (the optional iCloud backup below is separate).

Camera and photo library

AxleSet asks for camera or photo-library access only when you tap to scan or attach a photo to a scale ticket. The photo is copied into the app's own storage on your phone. It is not uploaded or shared; the numbers are read from it on your phone. Like every app's documents, it is included in your own iPhone backup if you keep one, and AxleSet's own iCloud backup never carries photos. You can use the whole app without ever granting either permission.

Location

AxleSet asks for your location only when you open CAT Scale locations near me, to sort the list by distance. The position is read once on the phone, used for that list, and not stored or sent anywhere. You can use that screen without location by searching a CAT number or truck stop name, and the rest of the app never asks. You still pick your state yourself.

Reading a ticket photo

When you scan a scale ticket, the numbers are read from the photo on your iPhone by Apple's built-in text recognition. The photo is not uploaded anywhere.

iCloud backup (optional)

If you turn on Back up rigs and tickets to iCloud, AxleSet writes one file to your own iCloud Drive container for this app. What goes in it: your rigs, your tickets (weights, dates, places and the road class saved with each one — not the photos), the state you picked, whether you were on the Interstate, a state highway or one of a state's lower road classes, your slide strategy and drives target, and pounds or kilograms. What never goes in it: the ticket photos, and the last setting in the list above, which stays on this phone only.

Restoring that copy replaces what is on the phone: your rigs, your tickets and those settings are swapped for the ones in the copy, and anything on the phone that is not in the copy is gone with it — the photos already on this phone stay with their tickets. It goes through Apple's iCloud under your Apple ID; we have no server and cannot see it. The switch only stays on if that first copy actually goes up: if this iPhone has no iCloud account signed in, AxleSet turns it back off and tells you, instead of leaving you thinking there is a copy. Turn it off any time; delete the app's data from iCloud Drive in Settings.

Internet

AxleSet makes no network requests of its own. The state limits, fine schedules and the CAT Scale list are built into the app. Tapping a scale opens catscale.com or Maps in their own apps. The optional iCloud backup uses Apple's iCloud, nothing else.

The scale list

Scale locations come from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) and link to catscale.com by scale number. Nothing about which scales you look at is recorded.

Sharing a ticket

When you tap share on a ticket PDF or export your tickets as CSV, the file goes only where you send it (Mail, Messages, Files, your dispatcher's app). The PDF includes the photo you attached to that ticket; the CSV never carries photos. AxleSet itself never sends it anywhere.

Purchases

AxleSet is free to download and use. AxleSet Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store and managed by Apple: Apple processes the payment, and neither the app nor we ever see your payment details, your Apple ID or your card. You cancel it in your Apple account settings, and the Restore button brings it back on a new phone. If you bought the earlier one-time unlock, you keep it, and Restore brings it back too. Whether AxleSet Pro is active, and until when, is stored on this phone, not on a server of ours — that is why the app answers the question with no signal and without asking anyone.

Children

AxleSet is a tool for commercial drivers and is not directed at children. Since it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.
